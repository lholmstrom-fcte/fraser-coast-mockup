Fraser Coast homepage concept

Changes in this revision

Nature’s Calendar presents all twelve months together to help visitors compare seasons. The homepage map supports zooming, dragging and selecting Hervey Bay, K’gari and Maryborough.

Reviewing the concept

Explore the navigation menus, calendar and map on desktop or mobile. The light and dark theme switch is at the top of the page. Feedback on the visual direction, clarity of information and ease of finding destinations will help shape the next revision.

Destination and category links lead to the development website and may require access. The trip planner and local guide demonstrate the proposed interface. Calendar seasons and editorial wording are subject to content review.
