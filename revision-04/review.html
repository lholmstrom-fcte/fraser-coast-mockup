Newsletter popup and responsible travel

The static deals strip is replaced by an offers and newsletter dialog after 45 seconds. It waits while another dialog, menu or form is in use, and appears once per tab session. Dismiss with Escape, the close button or the backdrop. The newsletter remains a local demonstration. The coastal section now includes a family 4WD campsite image and Queensland Parks care guidance.

New in revision 04

A restrained road-trip section follows the trip planner, with northbound and southbound destination order, three expandable Fraser Coast stops and a coastal detour link. The Plan Your Trip menu links directly to it. Routes are illustrative destination sequences, not turn-by-turn directions; no unverified driving times are shown. Museum access and operating days need confirmation before production itinerary publication. Previous revisions are preserved.

Map direction for discussion

This version uses the existing local interactive coastline map. Mapbox is a candidate for the production map, including custom brand styling and illustrated location markers. Both reference homepages loaded Mapbox resources when reviewed on 12 November 2026.

A production choice needs an estimate of monthly map loads, required location data, styling effort and accessibility. Custom illustrations can be added as image symbols. Forecast costs against Mapbox’s current pricing before selecting the service.

Later decisions

  • The newsletter-and-offers popup appears after 45 seconds and once per tab session. The permanent footer signup remains available. Both forms are local previews.
  • Blog page layouts will follow Sarah’s reference research. The homepage retains its stories and inspiration section.
  • The supplied whale photograph is 1200 × 800 pixels. A higher resolution original would improve sharpness on large and high density displays.
  • Destination and category links lead to the development site and may require access. The trip planner and local guide remain interface previews.
